## Restart policy, afterwards
docker inspect after the kills:
  fault-monitor  status=exited exit=137 restarts=0 policy=unless-stopped
  supervisor     status=exited exit=137 restarts=0 policy=unless-stopped
A kill through the daemon is recorded as a manual stop, which suppresses the policy.
PID 1 inside the images is 'java -jar quarkus-run.jar' with no init wrapper, and SIGKILL
sent to PID 1 from inside its own namespace is ignored by the kernel, so the policy cannot
be exercised from outside. It remains asserted by configuration and not demonstrated.

## Restart policy, measured
docker exec <container> kill -TERM 1   (SIGTERM from inside; Quarkus handles it)
  before: status=running restarts=0 policy=unless-stopped
  t+3s:   running 1
  after:  status=running exit=0 restarts=1
The policy fires when the process exits on its own. It does not fire after docker kill,
because a daemon-issued kill is recorded as a manual stop. A chaos test that only uses
docker kill cannot tell a working restart policy from a missing one.
